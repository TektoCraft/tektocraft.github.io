Kyudo Notebook

Log kyudo hits and misses arrow by arrow. Charts show hit rate and totals.

Download on the App StoreGet it on Google Play

Screenshots

A simple notebook for kyudo (Japanese archery) practice. Tap "Hit" or "Miss" for each arrow you shoot, and see your results and hit rate for the day right away. Daily and monthly arrow counts, and your running totals, are shown in charts.

What you can do

  • Record each arrow with the "Hit" and "Miss" buttons, in the order you shot. Made a mistake? "Undo" removes the last arrow
  • Records are shown in pairs of two arrows, with the traditional marks (double circle, circle, cross), so they are easy to read
  • See "hits / total" and your hit rate for the day at a glance
  • Arrows per day: a stacked bar chart (miss and hit) for each day of the month
  • Arrows per month: a stacked bar chart for each month of the year
  • Running total by day and by month: choose a starting month (or year) and see the total number of arrows you have shot
  • Up to 240 arrows can be recorded per day
  • Back up and restore your records (JSON file)

Good for
Members of kyudo clubs, people who train at a dojo, and anyone who wants to follow their hit rate toward a grading or a tournament. Seeing your practice add up in numbers and charts keeps you motivated.

About your data
Your records are stored on your device. No account is needed. If Android's automatic backup is on, your records may be restored when you switch phones.

Notes

  • Ads are shown at the bottom of the screen.
  • You may be asked for consent about ads when you first open the app. You can change your choice any time from "Privacy settings" in the menu.
