Scientific Calculator Standard

Trig, logs, roots and powers. Recall and edit past expressions to recalculate.

Download on the App StoreGet it on Google Play

Screenshots

A simple scientific calculator where you type the whole expression and see it on screen. If you spot a mistake, move the cursor and fix it right there.

What it can do

  • Basic arithmetic and parentheses
  • Trig functions (sin, cos, tan) and inverse trig (asin, acos, atan)
  • Common log (log) and natural log (ln)
  • Squares, powers and square roots
  • The constants pi and e
  • Scientific notation (x10^n)
  • Ans, to reuse the previous answer
  • Degrees (Deg) and radians (Rad)

Recalculate from history
Every expression you calculate is kept in the history. Bring it back with the Prev / Next buttons, tweak it and calculate again. (History lasts while the app is open.)

Good for
Homework, exam prep, science classes and quick calculations at work.

Notes

  • Ads are shown at the bottom of the screen.
  • You may be asked for consent about ads when you first open the app. You can change your choice any time from "Privacy settings" in the menu.
