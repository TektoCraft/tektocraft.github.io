Class Time Table

A simple timetable with a widget. Color-code classes and count attendance.

Download on the App StoreGet it on Google Play

Screenshots

MyClassTime is a simple timetable. Tap a cell for a day and period and enter your class. Add the teacher and room, pick a color to tell classes apart at a glance, and count your attendance, absences and late arrivals for each class.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

  • Choose how many days (5 to 7) and how many periods per day (5 to 10)
  • Enter class name, teacher, room and a memo for each cell
  • Pick a color for each class from 42 colors
  • Count attendance, absences and late arrivals with plus and minus buttons
  • Keep up to 10 timetables, for example for each semester, school or club. Swipe left or right to switch, and rename each one
  • "Copy from" puts the same class in more than one cell. Copied cells share their memo, attendance counts and color, so a class that meets twice a week is counted as one ("Unlink copy" makes it a separate class)
  • Back up and restore your timetables (JSON file)

WIDGET

Add the "Today's Classes" widget to your home screen to see today's classes by period at a glance. You can resize it, and it follows the dark theme.

GOOD FOR

Middle school, high school, college and vocational students, and for planning cram school or lessons. Counting attendance helps you keep track of credits and required attendance.

ABOUT YOUR DATA

What you enter is stored on your device. No account is needed. If Android's automatic backup is on, your data may be restored when you switch phones.

NOTES

  • Ads are shown at the bottom of the screen. You may be asked for consent about ads when you first open the app. You can change your choice any time from "Privacy settings" in the menu.
