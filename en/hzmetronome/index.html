Hz Metronome

A metronome you can set in BPM or Hz. A lock prevents accidental taps.

App Store: coming soonGoogle Play: coming soon

Screenshots

Hz Metronome is a metronome whose tempo you can set either in BPM or as a frequency in Hz.

BPM or Hz

Set the tempo in BPM (20–360) or as a frequency in Hz (0.33–6.00, in 0.01 steps), for example 1.83 Hz. The other unit and the period in seconds are always shown. Handy as a reference rhythm for walking, running or jumping, and for vibration experiments.

Steady beats

A whole bar is generated as one waveform and played in a loop, instead of being triggered beat by beat with timers, so the interval between beats stays steady.

Easy to see

Large dots show the beat. The whole background glows softly on the first beat (choose Off, First beat or Every beat). Vibration on each beat is optional.

Lock against accidental taps

Lock the screen so that the tempo and the start/stop button cannot be changed by mistake. Long-press the button to unlock. Safe to hold during a measurement or a practice session.

Features

  • 20–360 BPM with one-tap presets
  • 1–12 beats per bar and an accent on the first beat
  • Three click sounds (click, wood block, beep) and volume control
  • Option to keep the screen on while running
  • Portrait layout on phones
