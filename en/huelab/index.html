Hue Lab

Mix the target color. Train your color memory with a daily challenge.

How well can you see color?

Hue Lab shows you a color. You recreate it with three simple sliders: hue, saturation and brightness. The closer you get, the higher your score.

MODES

  • Mix – match the color you see. 5 rounds, 20 seconds each.
  • Recall – memorize a color for 3 seconds, then rebuild it from memory.
  • Daily challenge – everyone gets the same 5 colors today. Keep your streak going.

SCORING

Your result is measured with CIEDE2000, a color-difference formula designed to match how people actually perceive color. See your color difference after every round, earn a rank from Beginner to Color Master, and copy your result to share.

FEATURES

  • Quick: a full game takes about 2 minutes
  • Simple controls, no sign-up, works offline
  • Your best scores are saved on your device
  • Available in 8 languages

MORE

  • Hard mode: Only 5 seconds per round
  • Color vision support: Show color values (H / S / B)
  • This game is not a color vision test and cannot diagnose anything. Colors can look different on different screens.
