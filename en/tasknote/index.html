Task Note

Color-coded to-do notes with reminders. Sort by color, set a time, get notified.

Download on the App StoreGet it on Google Play

Screenshots

Task Note is a simple to-do notepad with colors and reminders. Sort your notes by color, set a time, and get a notification when it is time, so nothing slips your mind.

Sort by color

Pick one of 8 colors for each note. Give each color a name such as Work, Shopping or Study, and tap a color tab to see only that kind of note.

Never forget

Choose a time and get a notification at that time. In one tap you can set "in 1 hour", "tonight", "tomorrow morning" or "this weekend", and repeat daily or weekly. From the notification you can mark the note done, or snooze it for 10 minutes.

Check things off

Tick a note to mark it done. Swipe to complete or delete (and undo a delete right away). Hide finished notes, or clear them all at once.

Easy to read, easy to use

Sort by newest, by color or by reminder time. Switch between one-line and multi-line views. Dark mode and sharing are supported.

Your notes are stored on your device (Android automatic backup is supported).
