Daily Rhythm Practice

One-minute snare rhythm drills, from whole notes to two-hand polyrhythms.

Download on the App StoreGet it on Google Play

Screenshots

Daily Rhythm Practice trains your rhythm with a single snare sound. Just tap the screen. One minute at a time, easy to keep up every day.

1-minute drill (the same piece every day, so you can see yourself improve)

- Easy: the basics in one pass: quarters, eighths, triplets and more (one hand)
- Normal: a different rhythm in each hand (gentle)
- Hard: 3 or 6 hits per bar and alternating hands, a different rhythm in each hand (tougher)
Because the piece is the same every day, your score is your progress. See your streak and a chart of your best score per day.

Basics (one rhythm at a time)

30 lessons, starting from whole and half notes, with plenty of rhythm variations: quarter and eighth patterns, backbeats, off-beats, triplets and sixteenth-note groups. No endless runs. Listen to the example, then play. Earn 3 stars to clear.

Advanced (a different rhythm in each hand)

33 combinations of 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 hits per bar in each hand, such as 3 against 4 or 2 against 3. Notes fall straight onto the left and right pads, so you always see which hand is next (thumbs work well).

See your timing

Every tap is graded Perfect / Great / Good / Miss, and you see how many milliseconds early or late you were. The result screen shows your average offset and consistency. Use "Tap timing" to compensate for your device's audio delay.

Also

  • Tempo from 60 to 180 BPM
  • Countdown and an optional metronome click
  • 10 languages: English, Japanese, French, Spanish, Italian, German, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Hindi, Arabic

Free

Everything in the app is free, including the Hard 1-minute drill and every Advanced lesson. The app shows ads.

Note: sound plays through your device speaker or headphones. Some wireless headphones add delay; you can compensate with "Tap timing".
