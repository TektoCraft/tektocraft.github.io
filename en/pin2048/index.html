Pin 2048

A 2048 puzzle on an octagon board. Pin numbers so they never move.

App Store: coming soonGet it on Google Play

Screenshots

Pin 2048 is a number puzzle: slide the numbers, merge equal ones and make 2048. It differs from the classic in two ways.

An octagon board

24 cells in rows of 2, 4, 6, 6, 4 and 2. A roomier board that rewards careful planning.

Pin your numbers

Tap a number to pin it so it never moves (up to 3 at a time). Lock a big number in a corner, or leave a stubborn one where it is. A number that hits an equal pinned number merges into it, right where the pin is. How you use the pins changes the whole game.

How to play

  • Swipe to slide every number. Equal numbers merge
  • New numbers are 2, 4 or 8, so the game moves along quickly
  • Make 2048, then keep going for a higher score

Records

  • Your best score and the biggest tile you have ever made, always on screen
  • Confetti when you beat your best score
  • Your game is saved automatically, so you can pick up where you left off

Undo

Undo a move up to 3 times per game, free. When they run out, watch a short ad to get one more.

About ads

Free to play. There is a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and a full-screen ad when you tap "Play again" after getting stuck (at most once every 10 minutes).
