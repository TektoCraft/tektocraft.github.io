HealthLog

Log your day in 15-minute blocks. Review sleep, work, meals and weight.

Download on the App StoreGet it on Google Play

Screenshots

A simple life log: record how you spend your day in 15-minute blocks. Pick one of 11 activities such as sleep, work, meals or sport, and set the start and end time. Weight and a short note can be saved for each day. Look back at your records in reports and charts.

What you can do

  • Record a day in 96 blocks (15 minutes each): choose an activity, then set the start and end time
  • 11 activities: sport, art, sleep, work, learn, hobby, shop, house, eat, move and social (time you have not logged is counted as "Other")
  • Save your weight (kg) and a note for each day
  • Summary report: daily average time and share for each month (pie chart)
  • Monthly chart and yearly chart: how the time for each activity changes (line charts)
  • Monthly and yearly weight charts
  • A list of all your notes
  • Monthly life log: see how you spent each day of the month, in color
  • A reminder when you have not logged for a while (you can turn notifications off in your device settings)
  • Back up and restore your records (JSON file)

Good for
Anyone who wants to review their sleep and work hours, see weight changes alongside daily rhythm, or keep up a daily record.

Notes

  • This app is for keeping a record of your day and weight. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice.
  • Your records are stored on your device. No account is needed. If Android's automatic backup is on, your records may be restored when you switch phones.
  • Ads are shown at the bottom of the screen. You may be asked for consent about ads when you first open the app. You can change your choice any time from "Privacy settings" in the menu.
