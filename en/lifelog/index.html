Screenshots

A simple life log that records your day in 15-minute blocks. Tap a button to color in what you were doing.

Log with a tap

Fill in 11 color-coded activities — exercise, creative, sleep, work, study, hobby, shopping, housework, meals, travel and socializing. Add your weight and a note for the day.

See your daily rhythm

Monthly summary (daily-average pie chart), monthly and yearly charts, weight charts, and a monthly life-log table that shows the whole month at a glance. Export the monthly life-log table and daily totals as CSV.

Easy to keep up

See your logging streak. A gentle reminder arrives if you have not logged for a while. “Same as yesterday”, “Usual weekday” and “Usual day off” fill in a whole day with one tap.

14 languages

English, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Arabic, Thai and Hindi.

Back up and restore your log as a file, so switching phones is easy.

Not a medical device and not intended for diagnosis or treatment. Your log stays on your device (and iCloud on iOS); nothing is sent to the developer. Free to use, with ads.
