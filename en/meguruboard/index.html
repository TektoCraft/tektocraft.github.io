Cycle Board

Today's plan at a glance. To-dos that reset daily, weekly, monthly and yearly.

App Store: coming soonGet it on Google Play

Screenshots

Cycle Board is a to-do board for plans that come around again: every day, week, month or year.

Opens on today's plan

Items from every board that are due today are gathered in one list: matching weekdays, today's deadlines and things carried over from last month. They are sorted by nearest deadline (today, in a few days, this week, this month, this year, someday), then morning, noon and night. Finished items sink to the bottom.

Track progress with a slider

Record progress like "Proposal 60%" with a slider, or make a simple check item (lunch box, trash). A bar shows how far along the whole board is.

Resets by itself: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly

Repeating items go back to 0 when the period changes. You can pick the reset day for each item: if year-end tasks slip into January, reset them on January 10. Monthly items you did not finish show up as "Carried over" in today's plan. Finish them period after period and the streak is counted.

Switch boards

- List board: items with progress sliders
- Weekly board: supplies, trash days and lessons by day of the week, with morning, noon and night headings
- Yearly board: pruning, fertilizing, cleaning, tax returns, month by month
Add, rename and delete boards freely. Sample items are included to get you started (lunch box, trash, homework, tax return, cleaning...). Delete them any time.

Pick several weekdays at once

When adding an item, choose "Every day", "Weekdays" or "Weekend" in one tap, or tick days one by one. You can also set a time of day (morning, noon, night) and a rough target date (in a few days, this week, this month, this year, someday).

Morning reminder

Every morning at the time you choose, a notification tells you how many items are planned today and shows the first ones. No notification on days with nothing planned.

Backup

Export all boards, items and settings to a single file. Keep it in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email, and restore from it when you change phones.
