ABAB finds the faces in your photos and, in one tap, covers the eyes or blurs the faces. Pick a photo (or take one) and tap a button. Everything happens on your device — your photos are never uploaded.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

  • Take a photo with the camera, or choose one from your library
  • "Hide Eyes": a black bar across the eyes
  • "Blur": softens every face in the photo
  • Use both together, and undo any time with "Original"

GOOD FOR

Protecting the privacy of the people in a photo before you post it to social media or a blog.

PREMIUM (YEARLY AUTO-RENEWING SUBSCRIPTION)
Premium lets you save your edited photos and removes ads. The price and renewal period are shown in the app before you subscribe. You can restore purchases at any time.

Payment will be charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings after purchase.

PRIVACY

Photos are processed on your device and are never sent anywhere. If you do not have Premium, the app shows ads (Google AdMob).
